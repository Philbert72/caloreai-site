Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Calore tracks what you eat from a photo. This explains what it collects, why, who else sees it, and how to get rid of it. If anything here is unclear, email admin@caloreai.app.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to create your account and send the sign-in codes.
  • What you tell us about yourself: name, age, sex, height, weight, goal weight and activity level. We use these to work out your daily calorie and macro targets.
  • Your weigh-ins, so the app can show progress over time.
  • Your meals: what you logged, how much, and when.
  • Photos of your meals, to identify the food and to show you the meal again later.

We do not collect your contacts, your location, or your activity in other apps. Calore contains no analytics or advertising trackers of any kind.

Who else sees it

  • Supabase stores your account, your data and your photos. Photos sit in private storage; links to them are temporary and expire.
  • LogMeal receives a meal photo when you scan one, to identify the food in it. They receive the photo, not your name, your email or anything else about you.
  • Our email provider receives your email address, so we can send the sign-in and password-reset codes.

That is the complete list. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Nutrition data is shared, your meals are not

Calore keeps one library of nutrition facts across everyone who uses it, so the same dish does not have to be identified twice. That library describes food, not people. Your own meal log, what you ate and when, is visible only to you.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting your account removes your profile, weigh-ins, meals and photos immediately.

Deleting your data

In the app: Profile → Account → Delete account.

Without the app installed: email admin@caloreai.app from your account's address.

Full detail, including the one thing we cannot delete automatically, is on the account deletion page.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted. Meal photos are kept in a private store that is not publicly readable. We never hold your password. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth, and signing in requires a code sent to your email as well as your password.

Children

Calore is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who sees it, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
