Delete your Calore account

Calore — calorie tracking from a photo.

Delete it yourself, in the app

The fastest way, and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Calore and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your email address.

Your account and its data are removed straight away. There is no waiting period, and nothing is kept in a recoverable state afterwards.

Or ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address your account uses and we will delete it for you. No reinstall needed.

admin@caloreai.app

We action requests within 30 days, and usually much sooner. We will reply to confirm once it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your name, age, sex, height, weight and goal weight
  • Your daily calorie and macro targets, and your app preferences
  • Every weigh-in you have recorded
  • Every meal you have logged
  • The photos you took of your meals

What we keep, and why

Calore shares one library of nutrition facts across everyone who uses it — what is in a margherita pizza, roughly how many calories are in a serving. That library stays. It describes food rather than you, carries no link to your account once your meals are deleted, and removing it would degrade the app for everyone else.

Meal photos uploaded before October 2026 are not attached to any account. We did not record who uploaded them, so we cannot single yours out — if you would like those removed too, say so in your email and we will clear them.
